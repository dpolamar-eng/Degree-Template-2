Who We Are

A Legacy of Academic Excellence & Multidisciplinary Research

Established over four decades ago, Vanguard University has grown into one of the nation's premier autonomous institutions. Our academic framework integrates rigorous theoretical knowledge with hands-on practical learning in AI, computing, life sciences, business, and creative design.

Recognized as a Category-1 Autonomous University by UGC and accredited with NAAC Grade A++, Vanguard offers students a vibrant 120-acre smart campus equipped with supercomputing centers, digital libraries, and incubator labs.
